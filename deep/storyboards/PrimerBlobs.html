New theories

Compression after experience

Notation is cheapest when it summarises what was watched. Aggression 4:36; Supply and Demand 7:24. If shown first, expect more replays.

Costs make predictions real

Each benefit ships with a cost so the guess is open. Natural Selection 2:14; Foraging 8:38. Guessable pauses will be skipped.

Failure names the next ingredient

Epidemic R0 then S; Aging selection-only then mutation. Terms will feel needed, not imposed.

Dignify then dismiss intuition

Aggression 5:28, 6:03; Voting 5:43. Acknowledged intuitions should leave fewer residual misconceptions.

Planted-error challenge

Sacrificing 1:38-14:24; Cheater 18:54. Learners who fell in recall the assumption better later.

Honesty is cashable

Green Beard 2:25 paid at 9:22; Aging 21:41. Disclosed choices will not lower trust.
