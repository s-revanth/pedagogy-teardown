3 Toy world

4 Brute force

Naive way, deliberately a bit wrong

5 Wall or need

Method fails or a flaw appears; ask what we would hope for

Hits the failure on screen and poses it as the viewer's design problem.

failure-shown, design-it-yourself

Putnam 5:24; Bitcoin 11:15

6 Stepping stone

Named provisional object, then a diff

Invents an almost-version and upgrades it in one or two stated changes.

minimal-definition-then-refine, named stepping stone

Fourier transform 7:35; Neural net 15:10

7 Notation

Symbol as shorthand for a picture already owned

notation-after-concept, notation-compaction

Calculus 3:14; Fourier transform 12:37

8 Run and grow

Same machine on 2-3 examples, then bigger

Repeats the operation while the case grows, so the pattern appears before it is named.

visible-repetition, simulation-as-argument

9 Undercut

Show where the story breaks, list open questions

Admits the motivating picture fails or is incomplete.

failure-shown, honest-caveat

Gradient descent 13:09; Transformers 15:22

10 Callback and teaser

Close the hook, point at the next

Returns to the opening object, then says what comes next.

callback-to-hook, teaser-next, recap
