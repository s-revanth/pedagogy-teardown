## Strongest moves
1. **Add one rule, then watch (0:15-1:03).** The lonely bunny with a stronger urge to mate than to eat is a clean motivator for adding a female: the need appears before the fix.
2. **Visible sensory radius (0:47).** Shows what the bunny can see and that beyond it he is blind, which makes wandering versus homing behaviour legible without words.
3. **Simulation as argument (2:40-3:32).** Population spikes, crashes and recovers; then gene curves. Each trend gets a one-line reason (gestation steady, sight rises, speed not worth the hunger cost). Reasons follow plots, hedged with "I guess".
4. **Repeated runs (4:11-5:45).** Foxes first kill rabbits to extinction, then a second run with foxes from the start gives a rebound and a joint collapse. Different outcomes, one rule set, so chance and selection pressure become visible. Rabbits evolve higher urge under fox pressure (5:45).
5. **Humour and character (1:03, 6:06).** A joke about averting eyes and a happy-ending callback to the last surviving bunnies.
