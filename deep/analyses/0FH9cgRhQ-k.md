# Kurzgesagt: The Largest Black Hole in the Universe

## Arc
A single-axis ladder. After a paradoxical hook (0:00) and a scope cut (0:20, "how they work" is deferred to earlier videos), the video climbs five tiers: primordial, stellar, quasi star (hypothetical bridge), supermassive, ultramassive. At 10:33 it replays the climb, and an epilogue (11:25) admits the data limits. Merch closes it.

## Strongest moves
1. **Ruler-ladder (2:10-10:01).** Every black hole gets the same sentence shape (mass, width, landmark) and the landmark upgrades when it saturates: Paris, Corsica, Germany, then the Solar System. The viewer learns the template once, then each rung is a pure delta. Cheap on working memory, and only one unit (the solar mass) is defined.
2. **Gap as puzzle (4:01-5:38).** After a run of examples it notes nothing exists between 150 and millions of solar masses, and that growth by eating stars is too slow for the age of the universe. The quasi star is offered as the stepping stone, and flagged as unproven. The ladder gets a plot.
3. **Misconception-first (6:29-7:08).** Supermassive black holes are imagined as galactic suns. The video corrects this with mass shares (0.001% vs 99.86%) and says dark matter binds galaxies.
4. **Behind the Lies (11:25-12:56).** Admits only a couple of dozen black holes have good data, that mass comes from Kepler with large error bars, and that the Schwarzschild radius assumes no spin. This is the most unusual move: it undercuts the ranking it just sold, and so earns trust.
5. **Replay (10:33).** Recap as a second pass over the entire ladder, as the visible-repetition payoff.

## What's unusual
Honesty is placed after the entertainment, not inline. Notation is almost absent (the equation shows up only in the caveat). The list format is rescued by two real intellectual beats rather than by more facts.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder: a scale-ladder template, with a ruler object that swaps when it saturates.
- Orchestrator: schedule a gap or misconception beat every few list items to restore attention.
- Evals: check that any lesson with simplified models ends with an explicit caveat beat.
- Learner model: the replay pass is a natural place for a recall check.
