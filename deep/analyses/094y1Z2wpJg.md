# Collatz Conjecture (Veritasium, 22 min)

## Arc
Hook with a warning and an Erdos quote (0:00), literal hand-run of 7 to the 4-2-1 loop (0:16), conjecture and its many names (1:22), authorities warning juniors off (1:55), hailstone analogy with 26 versus 27 (2:13), randomness and Benford leading-digit histogram (4:14), the 3/4 shrink argument (7:13), directed graph and coral (9:10), two ways to be false and brute force to 2^68 (10:01), proof strategies and Terras to Tao (11:10), counterexample hunting, negative loops, almost-all versus all, Polya (13:22-17:00), Turing/undecidability (18:55), callback to Erdos and sponsor (20:31).

## Strongest moves
1. **Act it out (0:16-1:22).** Seven is iterated aloud, every arithmetic step spoken. The learner holds the whole procedure before any name or notation appears.
2. **Misconception then repair (7:13-8:40).** States the naive intuition (odd triples, even halves, so growth), then shows odd steps are really 3/2, with 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 chances of further halving, so the average is 3/4. Closes with 341 to 1024 as a concrete check.
3. **Visible repetition (5:00-6:00).** The leading-digit histogram is built sequence by sequence for seeds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and then settles. Learner sees the shape form.
4. **Evidence then undercut (10:18-17:48).** 2^68 checked, loop must be 186 billion long, then perfect squares (15:19) and Polya's 10^361 counterexample show why checking is weak. Turing tape (16:41) gives a picture of a finite tested prefix.
5. **Stakes via authority (1:55-4:00).** Experts warn against working on it, which makes a school-level problem feel dangerous.

## Unusual
Most explainers resolve. This one ends in honest uncertainty and possible undecidability, and spends a third of its time on why confidence is unwarranted. It also uses real stock-market and Benford detours as outside connections, with a caveat that Benford cannot settle the question (6:57).

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12. Partly: 2, 3, 7, 8, 13. Notation stays verbal for a long time. Speech rate was not measurable here; idea rate slows during the 3/4 argument and accelerates in the Tao section, which supports principle 8 only weakly.

## Velocity implications
- Build a hand-iteration scene that steps then speeds into a batch run with a growing histogram.
- Probe the learner with a predict-then-reveal on the grow versus shrink misconception.
- Add an orchestrator rule: after evidence, teach its limits.
- Eval: does the tutor distinguish evidence from proof.
- Use authority or stakes as a hook when the content itself is simple.
