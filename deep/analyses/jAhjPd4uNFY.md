# Kurzgesagt: Genetic Engineering Will Change Everything Forever (CRISPR)

## Arc
Stakes first, mechanism second, consequences third. A future-shock hook (0:00) leads to a history of tinkering (0:36-3:04), then the CRISPR mechanism (3:46-5:17), applications (5:51-7:43), heritable edits (7:43-12:11), and then ethics and risks (12:11-14:50). The close is a personal challenge to the viewer. The mechanism is only about 90 seconds of a 16-minute video.

## Strongest moves
1. **Future-shock hook with callback (0:00, 8:39).** A 1980s person told about smartphones would find it absurd; embryo editing is compared to the 1970s computer later. It calibrates trust in an outlandish claim using something the viewer lives in.
2. **Mechanism as a heist story (3:46-4:56).** Phages hunt, bacteria save a piece of the virus in an archive, make an RNA copy, and arm a protein that scans and cuts on a perfect match. It uses agents with goals and no notation. The viewer can retell it.
3. **Map vs GPS (5:17).** One short analogy carries the key idea, that the system is programmable.
4. **Consequence ladder (5:51-11:49).** HIV, cancer, single-letter diseases, heritable edits, aging, space. Each step reuses the same structure (what, status, timeline), and the slippery-slope list at 9:44 lets the viewer arrive at the conclusion by questions.
5. **Confrontation (12:11-12:48, 14:50).** It names the viewer's fear, says we already pre-select (92% figure for Down syndrome in Europe), then asks if the viewer would have been allowed to exist. It commits to a stance (participate, don't ban, 14:30).

## What's unusual
It argues a position. Honest caveats (aging skeptics, wrong edits, dark-state scenarios) are interleaved with optimism rather than parked at the end. Little structure of tiny-to-bigger examples inside the mechanism itself; rigor is minimal.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder: actor-and-goal scenes for first exposure to a mechanism.
- Orchestrator: choose a stakes hook when motivation is low; the callback gives a closing rhyme.
- Voice: affect-heavy topics need acknowledge-then-reframe beats.
- Evals: persuasion lessons should log claim, caveat and source per beat.
