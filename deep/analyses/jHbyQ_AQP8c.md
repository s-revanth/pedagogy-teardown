# What If Everyone JUMPED At Once? (Vsauce, 7 min)

## Arc
Hook question (0:00), Earth rotation via skater analogy (0:16), crowd made visual with Texas and Los Angeles (1:13), verdict that Earth barely moves (1:52), a misconception test of seismic effect with the BBC 50,000-person jump (2:52), energy comparison (3:28), a decimate tangent (3:45), a Dunbar's-number tangent about YouTube views (4:26), then a Newton's third law reversal (5:54) and a callback close (6:29).

## Strongest moves
1. **Make the crowd visible (1:13-1:52).** Before any physics, the video fits humanity into Texas at city density and then into Los Angeles standing shoulder to shoulder. The learner gets a concrete picture of the thing being tested. This is toy-world setup done by unit conversion.
2. **Absurd-unit smallness (2:14-2:31).** Earth moves about a hundredth of a hydrogen atom. The number is meaningless until it is expressed against something the learner already finds tiny.
3. **Empirical anchor for the misconception (2:52-3:28).** Rather than reasoning about seismic effects, it cites a real 50,000-person experiment reading 0.6 on the Richter scale, then scales to the Japan quake (7 million times more people).
4. **Deflate then reverse (5:54-6:46).** After repeated 'we are nothing', Newton's third law says you do pull the Earth, by a billionth of a proton. This calls back to the hook and leaves the viewer with agency.
5. **Dunbar perspective shift (4:26-5:38).** It uses a lifetime of meeting 2-3 people a day, a brute-force count, to size 100,000 views.

## Unusual
Two tangents (decimate, Dunbar) are only loosely related to the question. They are held together by the theme of crowds and power. No notation anywhere. Michael's pacing is quick and flat; the video relies on idea rate and quick anchor swaps.

## Principle verdicts
1 confirmed (skater, Texas/LA). 2 confirmed (no symbols). 4 partly (analogy never becomes rigor). 6 partly (lifetime counting). 7 contradicted (answers are given). 8 partly (flat speech, many fast ideas). 12 confirmed (BBC, Dot Physics, Straight Dope, Dunbar). 3, 5, 10 not observed or only weakly.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder: scale-ladder scenes comparing a quantity to a sequence of familiar objects.
- Orchestrator: support arcs that deflate a hope and then reverse with a true statement.
- Voice: tangents work if tied back by a callback; the orchestrator should track open threads.
- Evals: every number should be paired with a human-scale anchor.
- Caveat: this is a low-rigor explainer; its transferable value is framing and anchoring, not derivation.
