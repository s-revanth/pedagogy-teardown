# Try to Rule Fairly and You'll Be Overthrown (CGP Grey, 18:13)

## Structure arc
Second-person hook and paradox (why do rulers act stupidly?, 0:00-0:31) -> core model: no one rules alone, keys to power, treasure (0:47-1:52) -> three rules on dictatorship (1:52-6:11) -> same rules on democracy (6:11-10:13) -> taxes, productivity, resource curse, valley of revolution (10:29-16:49) -> generalize to CEOs and HOAs, zeroth rule (16:49-18:13). Music cues mark each chapter.

## Strongest moves
1. **One model, replayed.** Keys and treasure (1:04-2:09) plus three numbered rules are introduced once, then reapplied to democracy (6:57-8:36), resource states (12:26) and corporations (17:20). Each new section is a prediction test, not new material.
2. **Paradox resolved by incentives.** The opening asks whether rulers are stupid (0:15). The answer recurs: benevolent spending arms rivals (2:56), democracies are better because incentives align, not because rulers are better people (12:10). The paradox is the spine.
3. **Counterintuitive cases explained by the model.** Coup leaders purging allies (5:06) and the army letting crowds storm palaces (14:19) look irrational but fall out of rule 3.
4. **Valley of revolution (13:14).** Two stable extremes with an unstable middle: a shape learners can hold.
5. **Zeroth rule ending (17:37).** It recasts the whole list as aimed at the viewer and closes the opening role ("you want to rule").

## What is unusual
At 18 minutes it carries only one coined term ("keys to power") and one chart (10:29). The video stays light on notation and heavy on reapplying the model. The second-person imperative voice ("you must") turns descriptive political science into instructions, which makes the cynical claims feel discovered rather than asserted. Skeptically, evidence is thin: one graph, a few asserted examples (farm subsidies), and almost no honest caveats beyond the tax-average note at 10:46.

## Velocity implications
- New playbook "model then replay": teach a small rule set, then switch domains and ask the learner to predict before revealing.
- Mastery signal = unprompted correct application of the model in a new domain.
- Scene: persistent rule sidebar highlighting the active rule.
- Use distinct audio/visual chapter cues on domain switches.
- Evals should flag claims supported only by assertion, a weakness this video models.
