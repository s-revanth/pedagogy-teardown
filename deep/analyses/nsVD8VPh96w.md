# Simulating Foraging Decisions (Primer, 10:22)

## Arc
Meet the blob and its forest (0:00). Split the decision into two parts: what is possible, and what makes it happiest (0:32). Act out the extremes, graph them, name the frontier (0:51-2:29). Give every combination a made-up happiness score, add diminishing returns and a caveat (3:02-5:46). Add wood and get a surface (5:46). Combine both and let the blob search (6:33). Three predict-and-check scenarios (7:46-9:40). Teaser for trade.

## Strongest moves
1. **Act out, then plot** (0:51-2:29). The blob collects only mangoes (17), then only wood (14), then 1, 2, 3 mangoes plus wood. Points appear one at a time and only then does he name the production possibilities frontier. Concept before notation, brute force before the named idea.
2. **Made-up numbers on purpose** (3:34-4:07). "We're making this up, so let's say 100." Second mango 60. Telling the learner the numbers are scaffolding lowers the symbol cost, and the log function is waved off as optional (4:40).
3. **Caveat the assumption** (5:14). The utility function is an assumption; counter cases are named (taste awakening, overeating). A minimal definition is shown with its limits.
4. **Counterintuitive predictions** (8:02, 8:55). Double the mango value or yield, predict. The answer is not twice as many (13/6 and 12/10). Learner discovers the interaction, then gets a pricing analogy (9:40) to explain why.
5. **Pause-and-rewind permission** (6:17). "I've thrown a lot at you, pause, rewind." Pacing control handed to the learner.

## Unusual
- Analogy comes last, as a justification, not first as an entry.
- The blob "learns by trying" (6:49) instead of being solved analytically, with the reason stated.
- Humor (Taco Bell) carries the intuition.

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11. Partly: 3, 4 (analogy after, not before), 8 (speech flat; slow-down is sims and pause permission), 12, 13. None contradicted.

## Velocity implications
- Ghost hands plot each result point as it happens; name curves afterwards.
- Pick prediction cases where the obvious scaling guess fails.
- Voice flags invented numbers and assumptions.
- When the idea rate spikes, emit a recap or a pause permission.
- Analogy can be a late explanation tool, not only an opener.
