# Why Do We Kiss? (Vsauce, 12:16)

## Arc
Hook with strange numbers (0:00), health benefits (0:39), Pacman aside (1:17), kiss-feeding (1:36), saliva as mate test (2:49), selection story (3:23), baby vision and face detection (3:44), staring myth (5:39), food theory of love (6:18), Harlow (6:51), rejecting mothers and ducklings (8:12), puppies and uncertainty (9:14), Polarity Principle (10:06), back to kissing (11:01).

## Strongest moves
1. **Paradox chain (8:12 to 10:26).** Rejecting mother, shocked ducklings, random-treated puppies: the same counterintuitive result in three species. By the third, the viewer can say the rule.
2. **Set up the toy world (6:34 to 7:51).** Two mothers, one cloth and one wire. The viewer can guess, and the scare test settles it.
3. **Honest caveats (1:17, 5:58, 6:51).** Pacman is half true, psychic staring is pseudo-science, Harlow is unethical.
4. **Humor and the gross factor (0:19).** "Were they just being gross" makes the strange normal.
5. **We are the third group (10:41).** Puts the learner into the experiment.

## Unusual
Winding path from kisses to monkeys: the real subject (attachment under uncertainty) only emerges in the last two minutes. The bridge back at 11:01 asserts more than it shows.

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 1, 2, 9, 11, 12. Partly: 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13. Not observed: 3, 6. Speech rate does not slow; the long beats at 8:12 to 10:41 carry the novelty.
