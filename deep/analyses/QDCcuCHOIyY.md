# The Power of Suggestion (Vsauce / Mind Field, 29 min)

## Arc
Prestige and a teaser that the MRI is a sham (0:06), placebo defined and varied (1:37-3:15), three children introduced (3:15-6:35), deception layers and neuroenchantment explained (6:50-8:45), sessions (8:45-15:02), the hair dryer origin and the adult mind-reading demo (15:02-21:49), dogs (22:06), six-week results (23:15), ethics and the surprising twist that placebos work even when known (27:08).

## Strongest moves
1. **Open with the claim that is false (0:38-0:54).** The study seems to read minds; seconds later the sham is revealed and the real topic is faith. It is a hook-paradox that sets up the entire thesis.
2. **Let a real person be fooled (15:38-19:05).** The adult subject goes through a fake calibration, a staged miss (18:17) and a hit. The viewer watches belief form before the reveal at 21:14. The staged miss is explained afterward as realism.
3. **Show the props (7:06, 10:10).** The researcher lists layers of credibility (lab coat, equipment, nurse, hallway, ten-minute wait), which turns an abstract mechanism into a checklist of visible things.
4. **Before and after tracking (3:15, 23:15).** Three kids are introduced with symptoms, then return with outcomes (arms, migraine charts, medication-free summer). The payoff is concrete.
5. **Honest caveat and twist (8:13, 27:08).** Ethics: the parents knew, the children heard it was suggestion. The final twist, that placebos still work after you learn, pre-empts the obvious objection.

## Unusual
It is a documentary, not an explainer. Instruction is mostly delegated to expert dialogue with staged conversations. Michael narrates and interviews rather than lectures. The concept definitions come early but are brief, and a coined term (neuroenchantment) carries the theory. Weak causal evidence: results are anecdotes from three children without controls, which the video does not caveat strongly.

## Principle verdicts
1 confirmed, 5 confirmed (the audience watches the experiment), 12 confirmed (research lab, dog study), 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13 partly; 2, 3, 4, 6 not observed.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder: support roleplay simulations where the learner is the subject of an illusion and gets the reveal later.
- Orchestrator: put mechanism naming after the lived demo; prompt for a guess first.
- Learner model: record the prediction, then compare to the truth.
- Ghost hands: reveal deception layers one at a time.
- Evals: persuasion-heavy lessons need an explicit limits beat; this video is weak there, so do not copy its anecdotal certainty.
