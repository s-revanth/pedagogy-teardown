# Navy Indoor Ocean (Veritasium, 20 min) teardown

## Arc
Awe hook (a football-field-size ocean indoors, 216 paddles). Control as the key idea. Wave basics (energy not matter, circular motion, wave base). Frequency and speed with split-screen. Dispersion focusing, superposition, standing and bullseye waves. Ship model scaling (Froude vs Reynolds). Wind waves, swell, rogue waves, spectra. Navy anecdotes and innovation. Sponsor.

## Strongest moves
1. **Isolate one variable (3:01-3:55).** Same amplitude, three frequencies, split screen: viewers see slope looks bigger and speed is slower before the claim is stated. Visible repetition of one change.
2. **Demo as argument (3:55-4:28).** Fast-to-slow wave sequence timed to converge and break; superposition is named afterwards (4:28). Concept follows experience.
3. **Expectation correction (7:31-8:34).** Derek says he expected Reynolds, then Froude is introduced with its formula and a worked scaling: 46x smaller, speed 1/sqrt(46), footage slowed 6.8x. A concrete number carries the formula.
4. **Learner-offered analogy (11:00).** Host proposes high frequencies fade like concert treble, bass travels; expert confirms. Two-way analogy check.
5. **Rogue wave demystified (11:16).** Misconception answered by reference to superposition shown 7 minutes earlier, so it pays off the earlier demo.

## Unusual
Sparse formulas, long visual stretches. Topics are stitched by the facility tour, and the later half (wind waves, spectra, anecdotes) is less tightly ordered than the first half. Ending lacks a callback; the sponsor ends it.

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 5, 11, 12. Partly: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13. Not observed: 3, 6. Principle 8: speech rate remains quick; novelty is paced by long unspoken demo footage.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder: parameter sliders and split-screen comparators; one variable changes at a time.
- Voice: voice the wrong expectation before the correct rule.
- Orchestrator: after a demo, ask the learner to propose an analogy, then confirm or break it.
- Later concept payoffs (rogue waves) should reuse earlier demos; track them in the learner model.
- Ghost hands: show the particle path and depth cutoff as overlay before the wave runs.
