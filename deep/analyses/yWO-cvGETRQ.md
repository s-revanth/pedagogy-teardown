## Strongest moves

2. **Option menu with tone cues (4:51-5:41).** Lost is frightening but exciting; hidden gets a broken hard drive of photos; remnant gets a clown car joke. Each option has a cost, so the third wins.
3. **Toy world for the key fact (5:58-6:50).** Laundry baskets fill a room; squeeze one more sock and it collapses; then adding more makes the room grow. Viewer infers that information tracks surface area.
4. **Analogy chain for one idea (7:06-7:41).** Pond ripples, ultimate hard drive, paper book to e-book each attack "same content, different encoding."
5. **Defer the hard part openly (9:21-9:40).** String theory and maths named, promised later.
