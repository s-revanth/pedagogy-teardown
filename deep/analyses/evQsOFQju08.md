# Is Your Red The Same as My Red? (Vsauce, 9:34)

## Arc
Hook with agreed colors (0:00), flip to color-is-an-illusion (0:19), the inverted-spectrum question (0:51), undetectable differences (1:12), alien who cannot feel pain (2:03), terms qualia and Explanatory Gap (2:57), Tommy Edison (3:13), Dennett's language hypothesis (3:56), honest non-answer (4:48), apes never asking (5:27), Theory of Mind and Sally-Anne (6:50 to 8:21), return to red (8:40).

## Strongest moves
1. **Notation after concept (2:57).** The alien story makes the feeling-versus-knowledge gap concrete, and only then do qualia and Explanatory Gap appear as labels for something the viewer already holds.
2. **Act it out (7:12).** Sally-Anne is told as a literal five-beat story with objects moved, and the viewer is set up to predict. Child failure at about 4 is stated plainly.
3. **Tangent as mirror (5:27 to 8:21).** Apes never asking questions seems off-topic, but it is the same error as the Sally-Anne child, and it loops back to the hook.
4. **Honest caveat and reframe (4:48, 5:07).** He says outright there is no answer and that being able to wonder is the remarkable part.
5. **Callback to hook (8:40).** The chocolate and red lines close with "I can ask".

## Unusual
No notation, no brute force, no simulation. It is almost entirely stacked cases. It ends on an open question and does not resolve it, which most explainers avoid.

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 1, 2, 5, 11, 12. Partly: 4, 7, 8, 9, 13. Not observed: 3, 6, 10. On 8, topic jumps are fast and speech never slows; density is handled by case-stacking.

## Velocity implications
- Support an open-question lesson shape: caveat beat, then reframe, with no forced answer.
- Let the scene builder reuse one pattern across several cases, with a predict-then-reveal pause.
- Hold jargon until the learner has expressed the idea; log which terms were introduced.
- Evals: check that each new term follows its concept, not precedes it.
