## Strongest moves
1. **Work backward from the shape** (1:09-1:42). Instead of presenting the logistic equation, he draws the curve he wants and reasons that delta must hit zero at N = 50. The learner can invent the next step.
2. **Smallest term that works** (2:01-2:34). Crowding should be small when N is small and large when N is large. The simplest such term is a constant times N. He reasons about why before naming it as the crowding coefficient.
3. **Honest disclosure of tuning** (3:05). The coefficient 0.001 was picked by looking ahead so equilibrium lands at 50, a presenter-was-wrong style honesty about what was designed versus discovered.
4. **Verify region by region** (3:38-4:25). Early (near exponential), middle (growth rate flat, not speeding up), late (slows to zero), above capacity (negative). Each region is predicted from the graph and then matched to the sim.
5. **Luck as a lesson** (5:13-5:54). Predict outcome of a three-species race; orange wins; yet the worst-stat green beats blue, a reminder that the most likely outcome is not guaranteed.
