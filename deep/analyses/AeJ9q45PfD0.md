# Aerogel (Veritasium, 12 min) teardown

## Arc
Hook: lightest solid, 99.8% air, with four stated questions (0:18-0:37). Then a chocolate-bunny experiment starts and is left running. History story (Kistler's 1931 bet) brings the problem: remove liquid from a jelly without crushing it. Mechanism in three steps (wash, swap to CO2, supercritical). Hands-on lab demos of each step. Then properties: blue tint, yellow against sky, insulation by Knudsen effect. Applications and a teaser.

## Strongest moves
1. **Split demo thread (0:37 to 6:06).** The bunny burn test is cut away from at 2:01 and 3:45 and rejoined at 4:02, 5:49 so the insulation claim is evidenced before it is explained (explanation lands at 8:48). Viewers see the result, then want the reason.
2. **Failure before fix (2:33).** The naive route (just evaporate) is stated with its physical reason, molecules pulling on the skeleton, so supercritical fluid is understood as removing that pull.
3. **Act it out (6:23).** A hairdryer takes CO2 to its critical point and the liquid surface disappears. The abstract phase concept is a thing you watch.
4. **Surprise then law (7:18-8:32).** Blue tint, then the Rayleigh 1/lambda^4 line, then the yellow-vs-sky twist, closed with the sunset analogy. The formula is the only notation and comes late.
5. **Misconception on the property (8:48).** Air-filled does not mean air-like; pores smaller than mean free path.

## Unusual
Two-person, documentary-style banter where the host asks learner questions (can I touch it) and the expert answers on camera. The explanation is spread over the demo timeline rather than a clean lecture block. Minimal rigor: Knudsen and scattering get one beat each.

## Principle verdicts
Confirmed: 1, 5, 6, 11, 12. Partly: 4, 7, 8, 9, 13. Not observed: 3, 10. Principle 8: speech stays brisk; slowing is by demos, not by speaking slower. Principle 7 is only partly learner-discovered; the viewer sees evidence but is told the cause.

## Velocity implications
- Scene builder needs a persistent demo scene that the voice can leave and return to with state kept.
- Always render the naive method failing before the fix.
- Hold formulas until after a surprising visual; present as a label for what was seen.
- Ghost hands: point to the exact edge or region where the effect starts.
- Evals: check that each mechanism step answers an observation shown earlier.
