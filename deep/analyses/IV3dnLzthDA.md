# Midgley and Patterson (Veritasium, 25 min)

## Arc
Hook with deaths and IQ (0:00), Patterson's anomaly (1:07-2:34), detour to Cadillac, knocking, octane (2:54-6:51), Midgley's search and tetraethyl lead (7:10-10:23), toxicity and ignored warnings (10:23-12:18), return to Patterson's cleanroom and Earth's age (12:34-14:00), tracing natural versus human lead via oceans, ice, bones, teeth (14:20-17:30), IQ, crime, death toll (17:46-19:38), Freon (20:14), Midgley's end, bans, callback (22:05-23:15), sponsor.

## Strongest moves
1. **Planted mystery (2:34 to 12:34).** The extra lead puzzle is raised, left alone for ten minutes while a parallel story runs, then answered when leaded air contaminates samples.
2. **Live demo (6:01-6:51).** Compressing 98 octane versus diesel in a piston makes the octane scale physical before it is generalised.
3. **Define a scale with two endpoints (5:28-6:01).** Iso-octane as 100, heptane as 0, then 98 octane as a 98/2 mix. Minimal, arbitrary, then applied.
4. **Predict then measure (14:57, 16:06).** States that a natural source gives flat depth profiles and that mummies should show about 100x less lead, then reveals 10x at surface and 1000x in bones.
5. **Honest caveat (18:03).** Raises spurious correlation on the crime curve, then cites international repeats and a teen arrest study.

## Unusual
Narrative drives almost everything. Science explanations are short, embedded in a person's goals. Brute-force is Midgley's additive search, each failure named by its flaw (smell, cost), which teaches the criteria for a good additive. Second case (CFCs) is added as a quick parallel.
