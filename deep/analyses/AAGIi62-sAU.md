## Arc

Ending shrugs (32:46).

## Strongest moves
1. **Tailor-the-pitch demo (7:31-8:19).** Wheeler's "pressure politics" is shown by repackaging Paddy for each audience. The mechanism is acted, not described.
2. **Familiar analogy (15:01).** Blockbuster and Kazaa piracy: nobody arrested you, so was it a law? Then the enforcement ratio (one agent per 70,666 Americans, 15:49) quantifies it. This is the best Principle 1 and 12 moment.
3. **Try-the-alternatives skit (11:18-11:51).** Water, knitting, kites fail, so the viewer predicts the bootlegging turn before it is stated.
4. **Running gag as structure.** Hoover and Miles (26:34, 28:41, 31:02) and "release the lions" recur at turning points; they double as status checks on the political situation.
5. **Shrug ending (32:46-33:01).** Two morals offered and undercut: honest-caveat via comedy.

## Unusual
Length and sponsor interruptions (0:00, 12:06) with the video re-entering via "now where were we" (13:12) plus an alliterative recap line. The starting claim (alcohol is American) is a misconception-first hook that sets up the irony of the ban. There is no notation, no tiny-to-big ladder, and a high density of one-off anecdotes (Remus, Olmstead) whose teaching value is color rather than mechanism.
