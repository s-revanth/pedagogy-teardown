## Arc

## Strongest moves
1. **Enumerate until absurd (5:08-6:46).** Collectors itemize taxes one after another: income, window, tithe, salt, then the 9-year-old's tears. "There's a tax for that" repeats. The viewer feels the burden (brute force plus visible repetition) before the summary at 6:46.
2. **Simulate the rule (8:34-9:20).** Each estate gets one vote; the 130k, 350k, 27 million populations are said aloud, then a vote is run. The viewer predicts the 2-to-1 result. Strongest discovery moment.
3. **Stated causal law (10:42-11:15).** Mood is compressed into a transferable rule: fear boils over into anger, anger into violence.
4. **Honest caveat (13:08-13:23).** Admits the Assembly chose to cheer violence and that some historians see this as the root of the bloodshed.
5. **Bridging question (6:46).** "What could push them over the edge?" sets up the famine as the answer. A mini-hook before the new idea.
