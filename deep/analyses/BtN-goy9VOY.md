# Coronavirus Explained (kurzgesagt, 8:34)

**Arc.** Hook with the outbreak timeline and a two-part question (0:16): what happens inside a human, and what should we do. Then a long mechanism half (0:34-4:37): virus, cell hijack, exponential spread, immune overreaction, severe disease. A pivot (4:37) rejects the flu comparison and introduces two futures, fast and slow. The second half (5:15-7:45) justifies behavior (soap, distancing, quarantine). It closes by calling back to the slope and the hands.

**Strongest moves**
1. **Scale ladder (1:10-5:34).** One causal chain climbs cell, tissue, patient, hospital, society. Each rung explains why the next one matters. The hospital-crunch beat (5:15) is what gives the advice weight.
2. **Immune system as the weapon (2:03-3:13).** Cytokines are yelling, neutrophils and killer T-cells are confused soldiers. The surprising claim, your own defense does the damage, is carried by one analogy repeated across two cell types.
3. **Honest caveats (0:34, 3:31, 4:37).** Surface survival is uncertain, severe share is unknown, death rate is hard to pin down. Still commits to a direction: more dangerous than flu.
4. **Commit-to-answer framing (4:56).** Fast vs slow pandemic names the stakes in four words, and the ending callback (7:45) reuses them.
5. **Concrete why for soap (6:30).** Fat shell dissolves. A mechanism makes a trivial instruction credible.

**Unusual.** No notation, no quiz, no brute-force pass. It is persuasion by mechanism. The call to action uses second-person duty (you depend on cashiers, they depend on you).

**Principle verdicts.** 1, 4, 10, 11, 12 confirmed. 2 confirmed (few terms, each glossed). 7 partly: exponential growth is told, not shown. 8: voice stays steady; slowing comes from caveat beats. 3, 6 not observed.

**Velocity implications**
- When teaching a decision or policy, build the mechanism chain first and place the action last.
- A rate-slider chart (steep vs shallow) is the natural simulation; the learner discovers the capacity line.
- Voice should carry explicit uncertainty beats rather than hedge inside sentences.
- Learner model: track which rung the learner can restate before moving up.
