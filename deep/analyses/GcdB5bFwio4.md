3. **Failure shown (7:44).** The hydrophilic sample is ruined on camera, which motivates the chemical swap (7:59).
4. **Minimal definition then refine (0:18-0:54).** Lightest solid, good insulator, then immediately that it breaks, setting up the design problem.
5. **Outside connection (6:16-7:44).** Museum cases and a Mars seismometer reuse one property.
