# What Will We Miss? (Vsauce, 9:49)

## Arc
Hook (6009 upside-down year) -> ladder of future events we'll miss, from the time pyramid through Chernobyl, supernovas, Andromeda collision -> pivot "future people will miss things too" (4:36) -> list of future losses (eclipses, Niagara, Rushmore, Saturn) -> flip to your own missed past (conception, birth) -> star light as the same age as you -> warm callback close.

## Strongest moves
1. **Hook as paradox with a tiny fact (0:02)**. A numerology curiosity establishes the "I won't live that long" frame used the whole video.
2. **Scale-ladder list (0:35-6:32).** Same sentence form repeated across 10 events; the viewer learns the pattern and each item costs less effort.
3. **Simulation plus caveat (3:15-4:36).** Sky now vs 2 and 3.75 billion years, then admitting you are watching billions of years per second and that a human life sees it frozen.
4. **Symmetry pivot (4:36).** "Don't get FOMO" turns the list over: they will envy us, so the loss is relative.
5. **Personalised closure (7:20-8:57).** A calculator gives the viewer's own conception week, then a star whose light left when they were born. A random-walk photon is analogised to gestation.

## Unusual
Almost no derivation. Teaching is by arrangement: a single frame, escalating scales, then returning to the self. Humour (Milkdromeda, stepchild) lowers the weight of enormous numbers.
