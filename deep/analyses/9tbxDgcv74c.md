# Kurzgesagt: Nuke in the Mariana Trench (9tbxDgcv74c)

## Arc
A 6-minute video built as: catastrophe hook (0:01), staging the scenario (0:18-1:26), a literal "pull the trigger" simulation (1:26-3:34), a fear-by-fear refutation (3:52-4:48), a callback to the answer, then a Patreon pitch (5:06).

## Strongest moves
1. **Fear census then deflate (0:01, 3:52).** The opening lists tsunamis, quakes, nuclear winter and orbit ejection, so the viewer's expectation is maxed out. The close revisits each and retires it. The misconception is the spine.
2. **Act it out in micro-time (1:26-3:14).** The narrator walks through microseconds, one second, then the bubble's tug of war with the water. The bubble grows, shrinks, regrows, then fragments. This is simulation-as-argument: the result (nothing happens) is shown as a causal sequence.
3. **Contrast with the familiar case (2:23).** The same bomb at the surface makes a 10 km fireball. The trench makes a 1 km one. The hydraulic press image carries the pressure idea.
4. **Benchmarks from the real world (4:28-4:48).** Earthquakes of equal energy happen a few times a year, and over a thousand nuclear tests did not shift the orbit. Evidence is empirical, not theoretical.
5. **Commit early (0:18, 3:34).** "Well, almost" and "it does basically nothing" are stated plainly before the details.

## Unusual
No notation, no numbers beyond a few scale facts, and almost no definitions. It teaches intuition about scale rather than a mechanism you could reuse. Humor ("very unlucky fish") keeps tone light during violent content.

## Velocity implications
- Orchestrator: elicit or voice the learner's likely wrong predictions first, then retire each with a benchmark.
- Scene builder: micro-timed phase scenes with a side-by-side surface versus trench comparison.
- Learner model: log which fears the learner voiced and revisit those in order.
- Evals: refutations should cite a real reference event, not assertion alone.
- Caution: this video has one run and no repetition, so it is weak evidence for principle 7.
