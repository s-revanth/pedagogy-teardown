## Recurring gaps
1. **No question in either hook.** Both open on an assertion (Peto 0:00; Loneliness 0:00-0:34). The report counts hook-question 8/12 but never says when. Loneliness's first question is the self-audit at 8:27.
2. **Callback and commit are mis-specified.** Neither video returns to its hook question. Both close on a future-stakes line mirroring a phrase from the first 40 s (Peto 0:00 vs 6:46; Loneliness 0:34 vs 10:28). Peto refuses to commit (6:29).
3. **Caveats are untyped.** Peto has three epistemic caveats, one after each candidate (3:50, 4:10, 6:29). Loneliness has one safety referral (9:52) and states health figures flatly (5:21).
4. **Analogy budget varies.** Peto uses four agent-like stand-ins; Loneliness uses one (1:23). The generator over-applied sustained metaphor.
5. **"No learner response" is contradicted, and speech runs ~146-155 wpm, not 170.** Loneliness runs ~85 s of second-person self-audit questions (8:27-9:52).

## Strongest proposed rules, ranked
1. **Topic and question-status switch.** Open question: a 2-item menu at ~36% runtime, an epistemic caveat after each, no commit. Psychology: stats hook, define-by-contrast misconceptions in the first minute, at most 1 analogy, you-as-ancestor, no named avatar. (channel-specific)
2. **Advice mirrors the diagnosed loop**: one question set per node, 20-30 s each, then a referral line (Loneliness 7:02-9:52). (likely universal)
3. **Typed caveats**: epistemic after each candidate; safety referral at the end of advice. (channel-specific)
4. **Ladder replay as test**: reuse the setup's three rungs once later as evidence (Peto 1:54, 6:11). (likely universal)
5. **Closing-phrase mirror** in place of "callback to the hook". (likely universal)
6. **Assertion hook, paradox named at ~30% runtime** (Peto 2:14). (channel-specific)

## What the report needs to condition on topic type
A `topic_type` tag per video; per-type values for hook form, analogy count, stand-in kind, evidence kind, advice shape, caveat type and ending type; a solved-versus-open flag; publish year; and 4-6 psychology or self-help videos from the same channel.

## Verdict
The hypothesis generalises moderately inside its own topic type and poorly outside it. On a physical-world open problem it reproduced the arc, the timing (key beats within 40 s) and most moves. On psychology it kept the broad order but missed the hook form, analogy budget, advice shape and ending. Agency before mechanism, stakes before advice and no notation held in both. The weak spot is that conditional logic (open or solved, physical or psychological) is not written down and move counts are not trigger rules.
