## 6. Proposed rule changes

1. **Hook form for procedural topics.** *Trigger:* the title states a verdict and the subject is a legal or institutional procedure. *Action:* open with the plain factual question in the first sentence, then a through-line analogy (Congress's homework) by 0:16, and drop any staged misconception. *Evidence:* 0:00-0:16, 1:35. *Scope:* likely cross-channel (title-carries-thesis).
2. **Model as a quantified gag.** *Trigger:* the topic has a hazard or a probability. *Action:* install a numeric toy model (2D6, a seven means death) within 50 s and reuse it at least four times, once per risk point. *Evidence:* 0:47, 1:03, 1:18, 5:10, 8:26. *Scope:* channel-specific; the general form "one recurring device" may be universal.
3. **Ambiguity as two parses, then a worst case.** *Trigger:* a rule text with two readings. *Action:* show both readings as parenthesised logical expressions for 15-20 s, label it "interpretation problem", and later run a hypothetical that exploits the worse reading (5 steps). *Evidence:* 4:21-4:54, 8:06-9:17. *Scope:* likely cross-channel for law, rules and code.
4. **Repeat the cause label, not just the cause.** *Trigger:* each time a new case reveals the same flaw. *Action:* end the case on a recurring tag ("another interpretation problem", "President Ten all over again"), at least 4 times, and call back to the opening analogy in the last 60 s. *Evidence:* 2:09, 4:21, 8:06, 9:17, 9:35. *Scope:* likely cross-channel.
5. **Skip detail by joke.** *Trigger:* a list longer than about 8 items that the argument does not need in detail. *Action:* compress to a rapid sequence at the screen's pace with bracketed meta-asides ("no time") and give a single conclusion line afterward ("historical happenstance", 7:33). *Evidence:* 5:58, 6:47-7:33. *Scope:* channel-specific.
6. **End on an applied game.** *Trigger:* a procedural topic with a visible real-world event. *Action:* end in 20-40 s with an invitation to use the model on the event (State of the Union), no sponsor or teaser. *Evidence:* 9:50-10:29. *Scope:* likely cross-channel.
