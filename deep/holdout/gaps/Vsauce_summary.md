1. Answer-first thesis, with an exception within 20 s (both; channel-specific for now).
2. Late pivot of the title idea to the human condition, with a first-person number and one quote in the last 8-35% (both).
3. Open-mystery survey playbook: state, name, verify with one number, early caveat, three hypotheses (with a derivation, a false closure and a demo), synthesis (Zipf; likely generalises to unsolved topics).
4. Sanctioned detour: 2-3 min, up to 20% of runtime, one pivot sentence (both).
5. Barrage repetition: 8-14 one-clause instances in under 70 s (likely cross-channel).
6. Apparent violation, then dissolve by naming what was not carried (likely cross-channel).

## Verdict
The hypothesis generalises at the level of moves and voice (honest caveat, generalizing, story, minimal definition, named sources and the sign-off all appeared). It does not generalise at the level of arc and timing. The generator got the Zipf content skeleton (three mechanisms, the monkey refutation) from general knowledge, while the structural choices the report drove (brute force, callback ending, late notation, question hook) mostly failed. "Answer first, complicate, pivot to epistemic humility" looks like an unrecorded mode (two videos is a small sample). Fix arc order and endings and add the missing playbooks before using the report to regenerate a Vsauce video.
