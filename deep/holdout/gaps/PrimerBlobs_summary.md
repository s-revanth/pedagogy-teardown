## Strongest proposed rules (ranked)
1. **Format switch** between three templates: exploration sim, named-formula derivation, on-camera reflection. Each states which signature moves are on or off (cross-channel in spirit).
2. **Pain-driven second act**: when the by-hand method works but doesn't scale, state the cost, show the closed form, derive it a factor at a time (cross-channel).
3. **Checkpoint pauses distinct from prediction pauses**, one per 3-5 minutes before a dependent step (cross-channel).
4. **Numbered-lessons list with count roadmap and strongest last**, plus lay definition then label per item (cross-channel).
5. **Two-method verification at every worked case**, not only at the end.
6. **Dignify-then-dismiss extended to one's own past reasoning.**

- **Most wrong:** section 1 ("builds and runs a world before any theory"; "ends on a parameter change that breaks the model") and section 2 steps 4, 5, 9 as default arc steps.

## Verdict
The hypothesis generalises in values but not in structure. Primer's teaching DNA, small honest steps and named terms after concepts, appears in both new videos. The arc template does not, because it was fitted to 12 simulation videos of emergent behaviour. Fixing it needs conditional rules keyed on title and topic type, not more frequency counts. Caveat: n = 2, and one video is off-format.
