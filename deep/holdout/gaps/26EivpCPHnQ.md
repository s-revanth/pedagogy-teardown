## 6. Proposed rule changes

**R1. Cycle-first baseline (channel-specific; cross-channel for history).**
Trigger: the subject is the collapse or turn of one cycle in a recurring series (dynasties, empires, regimes).
Action: spend 30-45 s on a template of the whole cycle, one clause per prior instance, with a one-line "liked X" tag each. State the expected pattern, then show this instance fitting it, then flip with one flat line.
Evidence: 0:48-1:21 (Xia, Shang, Zhou, Qin tagged), then the deadpan corruption aside at 1:21. Later pay-off at 14:34-15:27.

**R2. Plant-and-pay-off means verbatim gag reuse, not causal facts (channel-specific, probably cross-channel).**
Trigger: any early skit or joke line (at most 15 words).
Action: re-run it 2-3 times at 3-15 minute intervals with the last beat changed. Prefer it over planting a causal object.
Evidence: the father's heart-attack speech at 1:54 and 4:57 with the twist that the uncle is regent; the "we know how this goes" aside at 14:52 pays off the eunuch plant from 1:37.

**R3. Add "full complexity, then zoom" when actors exceed about 6 (channel-specific; matches Kurzgesagt).**
Trigger: more than 6 named actors in the chain.
Action: drop skits and narrate 2-3 minutes at fast pace over a map. Before it, dare the viewer (a "keep up" dare at 9:33). After it, state the single thread to keep (12:11). Replaces the report's "no ramp" claim.
Evidence: 9:17-12:11 is about 25% of runtime.

**R4. Replace "modern relatable hook" with "in-period vignette with anachronistic gags" (channel-specific).**
Trigger: a sponsor tag of 15 s or less has ended.
Action: open within 20 s with an in-period scene, using 2+ modern anachronisms, in second person if possible. Don't use a modern frame story.
Evidence: village scene 0:15; Alexas and swimming pools 3:14.

**R5. Demote roadmap and tonal pivot to conditionals (channel-specific).**
Trigger for roadmap: Part-1 or multi-part framing only. Otherwise use a "let me explain" mini-lecture at 1.5-2 min that defines the one structural term (here "court culture").
Trigger for pivot: only if primary-source material or modern stakes are quoted. Mass casualties alone are delivered as a flat dark joke (4:22).
Evidence: neither appears in 17 minutes.

**R6. Characterise a villain with 3-4 escalating petty offences, then a label (channel-specific).**
Trigger: a new tyrant or villain enters.
Action: list 3-4 offences with the last absurdly petty, and stack another adjective onto the epithet each time the name recurs.
Evidence: 6:43-7:16 (sword, shoes, bed, trotting); epithet stacking 5:31-6:25.
