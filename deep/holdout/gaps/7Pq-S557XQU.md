## 6. Proposed rule changes

1. **Hook form conditional on the title.** *Trigger:* the title states the claim. *Action:* open in the first 3 s with a sweeping premise or plain question about the general pattern, then coin a two-term frame by 0:20-0:40; do not stage a second-person situation. Use "you" as direct address only from about 3:30. *Evidence:* 0:03-0:56; compare V2 0:00. *Scope:* likely cross-channel.
2. **Replace "2-5 rules in 70 s" with "one cause plus a paired coinage in 60 s".** *Trigger:* subject is evidence about a trend, not itself a rule system. *Action:* by 0:60, install at most 2 coined terms (mechanical muscles and minds) and one named cost cause; restate the cause threshold ("only needs to beat us") at least twice. Use a rule list only for rule-based subjects (voting, flags, succession). *Evidence:* 0:20, 0:38, 2:18, 5:16, 10:14. *Scope:* channel-specific refinement; the general form "one cause" is likely cross-channel.
3. **Ladder of exemptions.** *Trigger:* argue-a-thesis video. *Action:* build 4-6 tiers ordered by the viewer's self-image and skill (physical, driving, white-collar, professional, creative). At each tier boundary state the exemption in the viewer's voice ("surely they're safe?"), then break it with 2-3 named instances and one number. Each tier gets a title card and 1.5-2.5 min. *Evidence:* 7:00, 9:03, 11:06, cards at 0:56, 3:31, 4:57, 7:00, 9:03, 11:23, 12:33. *Scope:* likely cross-channel (persuasive explainers).
4. **Caveat form.** *Trigger:* thesis video built on evidence. *Action:* hedge with inline concessions ("not immediately, not everywhere") and scope cuts ("beyond the scope of this video"), no labelled speculation fence; fence only for claims about unseen futures with no instance behind them. *Evidence:* 4:24, 8:11, 11:42; assertive 6:04, 12:33. *Scope:* channel-specific.
5. **Reverse the analogy instead of breaking it.** *Trigger:* a familiar case (horses) where the viewer's intuition is correct. *Action:* state the false-sounding claim about the familiar case first, then swap in the target ("swap horses for humans"). Break the analogy only if a disanalogy would change the conclusion. *Evidence:* 4:08-4:24; the generator's break at "horses did not vote" has no real counterpart. *Scope:* likely cross-channel.
6. **Beat density and ending.** *Trigger:* any Grey-style script. *Action:* make beats 15-30 s each with a new named instance every 20-30 s, speech about 180-190 wpm. For a grave topic end on a quantified stake (45% against 25%) and a call to action, with no sponsor or teaser. *Evidence:* 42 beats; 13:58-14:50. *Scope:* channel-specific.
